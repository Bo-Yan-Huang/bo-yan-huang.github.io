## About

I am an M.A. student in Economics at National Taiwan University and a research assistant at the C2L2 Lab, working with [Kuan-Ming Chen](https://kuanmingchen.github.io) and [Ming-Jen Lin](https://economicsatntu.wixsite.com/ming-jen-lin). I also work remotely as a Research Professional with [Alice Wu](https://www.alicewu.io) at the University of Wisconsin–Madison.

My interests are in labor economics and industrial organization, particularly how workers and firms interact over the life cycle, from schooling and working to parenthood and retirement. I am especially interested in why firms pay for training that workers can take to other employers, and how firms compete for workers through both wages and amenities. I combine large-scale administrative and survey data with structural models to study how policies such as subsidies, taxes, and regulations shape who benefits.
